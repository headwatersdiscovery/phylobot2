# Fonts (High legibility, Matrix, Broadsheet, Dim, Abyss and Science Fair skins)

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono, by the Braille Institute,
designed for low-vision readers. SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt`): free to use,
bundle and redistribute with the software. Served from here, never from a font CDN, so
pages make no outside requests. Latin subsets; other characters fall back to system
fonts. Loaded only when a skin uses them (`skins/legible.css`, `skins/matrix.css`).

VT323 (Peter Hull; a VT320 terminal face, OFL) gives the Matrix skin its headings.

The Broadsheet skin (`skins/broadsheet.css`): Source Serif 4 (Frank Grießhammer for
Adobe, optical sizes for small text) for its text, Playfair Display (Claus Eggers
Sørensen) for its headlines, and Cinzel (Natanael Gama; Roman inscription capitals) for
the home and About pages' titles. All OFL.

IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono (Mike Abbink and Bold Monday for IBM) are the Dim
skin's text and code faces; Lexend (Bonnie Shaver-Troup and Thomas Jockin, made for
reading ease) gives the Abyss skin its headings. All OFL.

Patrick Hand (Patrick Wagesreiter; neat hand lettering) and Caveat Brush (Impallari
Type; a fat marker) are the Science Fair skin's headings and home-page title. Both OFL.

`@fontsource/atkinson-hyperlegible-mono@5.3.0` and `@fontsource/vt323@5.3.0`, `@fontsource/source-serif-4@5.3.0`,
