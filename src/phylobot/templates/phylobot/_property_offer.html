Measure or recompute properties

PhyloBot's list of properties grows, so a job can be given one that did not exist when it ran — the ancestors do not change, and there is nothing to re-run but the measurement. One that has been measured can be drawn again with a new seed, which is how to see how much of a number is sampling noise.
