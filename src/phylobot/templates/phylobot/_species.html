What is sent, and to whom?

Nothing leaves this machine until you tick a box. Open Tree of Life (api.opentreeoflife.org) receives the species names and taxids in this table, and returns the species tree. NCBI (eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov), UniProt (rest.uniprot.org) and Ensembl (rest.ensembl.org) receive the accession numbers in your sequence names, and return each one's species. Sequences are never sent. Each service's terms apply. Species codes in UniProt names (IME2_YEAST), gene ids of model organisms and names like Homo_sapiens are read here, without asking anyone.
