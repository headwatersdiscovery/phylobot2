Test for Positive Selection (Branch-Site)

codeml's branch-site test (Model A against its null; Zhang, Nielsen & Yang 2005) asks whether some codons evolved under positive selection (dN/dS > 1) on the foreground branches you choose, compared with the rest of the tree. Choose the foreground from your hypothesis (a duplication, a change of function, host or habitat) before you look at results: a branch chosen because its free-ratio dN/dS or its changes look striking has already been tested by eye, and the p-value no longer means what it says.

The test uses this tree's branching order and the codon alignment: codeml re-estimates every branch length under its own codon model, so the model above does not affect it.
