Extant Sequence Reconstruction

How good are the posteriors behind your ancestors? ESR holds out a sequence whose residues are known, reconstructs it as if it were an ancestor, and compares the two. Extant sequence reconstruction (ESR)The held-out sequence is reconstructed with the same alignment, tree and model as your ancestors were. Over many sequences it shows whether a posterior of 0.8 is right about 80% of the time: the accuracy and calibration of the reconstruction, on your own data.Sennett & Theobald 2024.
