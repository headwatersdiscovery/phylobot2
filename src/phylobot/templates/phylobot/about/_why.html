Why ASR Matters Across Disciplines

  • For AI & Generative Protein Design: Ancestral sequences represent functional, evolutionarily filtered sequence diversity absent from modern databases. Reconstructed nodes provide hyper-stable starting scaffolds and high-confidence out-of-distribution data for training generative models and benchmarking fitness predictors.
  • For Gene Therapy & Vector Engineering: Tracing ancestral capsids and viral enzymes (e.g., AAVs, ancestral Cas endonucleases) uncovers robust starting templates with enhanced packaging efficiency, novel PAM specificities, or reduced pre-existing immune recognition.
  • For Evolutionary & Mechanistic Biology: Pinpoint the exact epistatic mutations responsible for historical functional shifts, allosteric transitions, and ecological adaptations without getting lost in modern neutral drift.
