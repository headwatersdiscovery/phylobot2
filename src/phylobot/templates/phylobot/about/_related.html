Ancestral sequence reconstruction has an active community, and PhyloBot 2 stands on its work. Several of these tools are ahead of PhyloBot 2 in important respects; the README compares them in detail.

  • FireProtASR and FireProtASR 2.0: a one-stop web server from a single sequence to ancestors, with homolog collection by EnzymeMiner. doi:10.1093/bib/bbaa337, doi:10.1093/bib/bbag436
  • EnzymeMiner and EnzymeMiner 2.0: exhaustive mining of enzyme sequence space; complementary to PhyloBot 2. doi:10.1093/nar/gkaa372, doi:10.1093/nar/gkag424
  • Topiary: dataset construction, quality control and gene/species tree reconciliation for ASR. doi:10.1002/pro.4551
  • GRASP: indel-aware reconstruction with partial order graphs (run by PhyloBot 2 as a gap-placement method). doi:10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010633
  • AncFlow: a Snakemake ASR pipeline for automatically identified subfamilies. doi:10.1101/2024.07.30.605920
  • ARPIP and indelMaP: reconstruction with explicit models of insertion and deletion. doi:10.1093/sysbio/syac050, doi:10.1093/molbev/msae109
  • Nextstrain / Auspice: interactive trees with mutations on each branch, for pathogens. doi:10.1093/bioinformatics/bty407
  • MEGA 12: a desktop toolkit including maximum-likelihood ancestral states. doi:10.1093/molbev/msae263
