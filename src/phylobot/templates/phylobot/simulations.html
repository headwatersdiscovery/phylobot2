Simulations

Evolve sequences along a tree from a root sequence. The true ancestors are known, so a reconstruction can be scored. SimulationsStart from a root sequence, let it change along every branch of the tree, and see what the tips become. Run a job on the result and its ancestors can be checked against the truth.Simulated with AliSim (Ly-Trong et al. 2022).
